## 2. Description of the App

MARCWILLS HRVA is a heart rate variability (HRV) analyzer designed for sport
science research, exercise physiology, and wellness monitoring. The App acquires
physiological data from a Polar H10 chest belt sensor via Bluetooth Low Energy
(BLE) and provides:

- Real-time HR monitoring and HRV analysis (Stationary Mode)
- Activity monitoring with Karvonen-based intensity guidance (Activity Mode)
- ECG waveform display and recording, an R-peak check against the sensor's RR
  intervals and a signal-quality index; Poincaré plot visualization
- Handling of sensor disconnections: a recording continues across a Bluetooth
  drop, and each gap is recorded and shown, never filled in
- Session summary reports with pre-post HRV comparison, also as a PDF
- Session markers (numbered events, body-position changes, segments)
  placed during a recording
- Export of session files through the system Share Sheet or into a folder you
  choose, an export of a whole session as one encrypted `.hrva` file, and an
  export of the App's complete database as a backup
